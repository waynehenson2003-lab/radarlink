      Radar detector (V1)

      V1 is the Valentine One radar detector. RadarLink reads its alerts over Bluetooth, then adds the map, voice and filtering.

      • V1 Gen2: Bluetooth is built in. Tap Connect V1 and pick the device whose name starts with V1.
      • Older V1: needs Valentine's V1connection LE, a small Bluetooth adapter that plugs into the detector. Pick the V1C-LE… device.
      • No V1? Skip this. The map, speed cameras and city filtering work on their own.

      Only one app can link to the detector at a time, so close Valentine's own app first.

        About

        Needs Chrome on Android over https://. iPhone and iPad can't use Web Bluetooth, so they get the map and cameras but no V1 alerts.

        To link your detector, see Radar detector (V1) at the top of Settings.

        Keep RadarLink on screen while driving, because Android delays sound from background tabs. Laser alerts are never filtered or locked out.
